About

Tried in the courtroom. Trusted in the boardroom.

Bill Barfield serves as the Managing Partner of Litigation at Porter Law Firm, leading a team of attorneys from the firm's Houston and Austin offices. His practice centers on construction defect litigation, where he represents developers, general contractors, subcontractors, and property owners in complex disputes involving residential and commercial properties.

With a rare combination of legal expertise and business acumen — holding an MBA alongside dual Master of Laws degrees — Bill brings a strategic, results-driven approach to every case. His clients rely on him not just to litigate, but to find the most efficient path to resolution.
